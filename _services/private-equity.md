---
title: 'Private Equity Syndication'
---

We provide end-to-end advisory solutions to companies in high-growth markets on their capitalization/re-capitalization strategies, which helps them to achieve their growth targets. Typical PE engagements go through the following phases:

## Phase 1: Collateral Preparation
In this phase, we study the company’s existing business profile and growth prospects. Based on our understanding of the private equity market, we help the client decide on the most effective capitalization strategy. We also help prepare a complete information pack that presents the transaction to potential investors in a structured way.

## Phase 2: Roadshow and Investor Shortlisting
In this phase, Banyan helps the client determine the right private equity fund profile, holds initial discussions with investors, supports roadshows with potential investors, and advises the client on shortlisting. We use our relationship network with private equity funds extensively in this phase.

## Phase 3: Commercial Term Sheet
In this phase, Banyan helps the client analyze offers from potential investors, drives deal negotiations, and supports selection of a term sheet that aligns with the client’s strategic objectives.

## Phase 4: Due Diligence and Closure
In this phase, we help the client coordinate the overall due diligence process and negotiate final definitive agreements with the potential investor.
